Key Takeaways
✔
Vape 4× Points is the clearest campaign win this August
Loyalty revenue grew +32% YoY while accounting for 68.3% of total vape. Non-loyalty dropped -29.2% in parallel — the multiplier pulled enrolled customers deeper into the category. That divergence is the signal: the campaign is working exactly as designed, concentrating spend on cardholders without depending on casual buyers to carry revenue.
📊
Fireworks: August is the wrong month to judge the campaign
The -55.7% August decline reflects end-of-season clearance, not a promotion failure. Fireworks demand peaks in June and July; by August, inventory is selling out at discount. The summer arc (May–August) delivered $433,069, up +49% versus the prior summer. The category is healthy — the timing of an August-focused push was structurally disadvantaged.
⚠
Food to Go loyalty is widening but spending less per visit
Cards grew +14.5% and trips grew +17.9%, but avg basket fell -5.6% to $8.68. More enrolled customers are visiting more often, yet each transaction is lighter. The 2× points offer successfully deepened engagement frequency — but frequency without basket growth limits revenue upside. A bundle mechanic targeting per-visit spend is the natural next step.
🚪
No new loyalty enrollments were activated in Food to Go
All 142 loyalty buyers in August 2026 were returning Grab N Go cardholders. The campaign deepened loyalty within an existing base but did not bring a single new member into the category — compared to 2 first-timers in August 2025. A points campaign alone does not convert non-loyalty shoppers; an active enrollment hook at checkout is needed to grow the base.
🚧
Bagel + Coffee needs a POS fix before it can be measured
TXP rings bagels and coffee as separate transactions, making it impossible to confirm combo purchases in the data. TGC confirmed 12 same-basket combos. Since these SKUs have no August 2025 history, there is no prior-year baseline to compare against. August 2026 is Month 1 — the offer needs a process alignment at TXP and a clean September run before any performance read is possible.
Recommendations
1
Re-run Vape 4× Points in Q4. This is the highest-ROI campaign structure in August. A holiday-season push — tied to a new SKU launch or seasonal brand — would leverage the same proven mechanic on a high-intent audience. SW1 outperformed (82 cards, $45K loyalty rev) and is the stronger anchor for Q4 targeting.
Q4 Action
2
Shift Fireworks promotions to May–June 2027. The summer arc data confirms strong category demand that peaks well before August. A Memorial Day launch with a loyalty multiplier would capture peak-demand traffic rather than closing out inventory after the season has passed.
2027 Plan
3
Add a bundle mechanic to Food to Go to lift avg basket. With trips up but per-visit spend declining, the gap is per-transaction value. A "Earn 2× points on a combo purchase" structure — pairing the existing Bagel + Coffee $4.99 offer with the Grab N Go points campaign — gives enrolled customers a reason to consolidate more spend in one visit rather than multiple lighter stops.
Q4 Action
4
Fix the Bagel + Coffee POS ring at TXP; set September as Month 1. Align cashier process at TXP so bagel and coffee ring together when ordered as a combo. September 2026 then becomes the first clean baseline month, enabling month-over-month tracking and a Nov 2026 vs Nov 2025 YoY comparison by end of year.
Ops Fix
5
Launch a loyalty enrollment prompt for non-loyalty Vape and Grab N Go buyers. Non-loyalty spend is declining in both categories (Vape -29.2%, Food to Go -6.3%). These customers are already at the register — a point-of-sale enrollment prompt ("Earn 4× points today — sign up free") converts existing cash transactions into cardholder data and expands the audience future campaigns can reach.
