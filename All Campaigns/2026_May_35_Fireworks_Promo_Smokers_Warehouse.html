Methodology

Five analytical lenses applied to evaluate campaign effectiveness.

1 — Revenue Lift

Compare fireworks category revenue and average transaction size in May 2026 vs May 2025. The tiered discount is designed to drive trade-up — a rising avg FW basket size signals customers are spending more to reach higher tiers. Revenue lift alone can mask a flat basket if more customers simply showed up.

2 — Tier Distribution

What % of FW baskets fall into each spend tier? If the majority cluster in Tier 1 ($50–$999) and Tiers 2/3 remain empty, the higher-value thresholds aren’t being activated — which may signal a need to reconsider thresholds, messaging, or in-store promotion visibility. Summer data (Jun–Jul) is the real stress-test for tier activation.

3 — Cost of Discount

For each tier: discount dollars given = discount rate × FW spend in that tier (10% / 15% / 20%). This is the direct promotion cost. The effective blended rate across all qualifying baskets shows ROI exposure. Comparing against historical summer periods gives a projected cost envelope for June–August 2026.

4 — Customer Reach

Using loyalty card data: what portion of FW buyers are new-to-file (never purchased before), infrequent (<12 prior shopping days), or regulars (12+ prior days, ~monthly+)? Seasonal promos should skew toward broadening reach — a high new-to-file % is a positive signal that the promotion is attracting non-regulars.

5 — Basket Analysis (Cross-sell)

Are fireworks buyers also purchasing other Smokers Warehouse categories? Cross-sell rate = % of FW baskets that contain at least one non-FW item. High cross-sell means FW buyers are higher-value total customers. The co-purchased category breakdown shows which departments benefit from the fireworks traffic.

6. Customer Reach — Who Are the Fireworks Buyers?

Loyalty card data used to segment FW basket holders. Segmentation is only possible for card-swiped baskets. New-to-file: card has zero prior shopping days in the dataset. Infrequent: 1–11 prior distinct shopping days (< monthly cadence). Regular: 12+ prior days (at least monthly). A seasonal promo should attract a meaningful share of new-to-file and infrequent shoppers — validating reach beyond the existing base.
