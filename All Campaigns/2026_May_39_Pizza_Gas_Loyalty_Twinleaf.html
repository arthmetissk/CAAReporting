1. Gas Purchase Distribution — $30+ vs <$30: Loyalty vs Non-Loyalty
Promotion mechanics: Loyalty customers who fill up $30+ in gas receive a free pizza slice. If the promotion is effective, the share of loyalty customers hitting $30+ should increase from April to May — and ideally grow faster than non-loyalty (which serves as a baseline with no promo incentive).

Loyalty = unique PatronPoints card holders per month. Non-loyalty = gas fill-up transactions (individual customers cannot be de-duplicated without a card).
