Methodology

How redemptions are identified in loyalty data:
1. Find all loyalty gas transactions (Department = NULL) with Amount ≥ $30.00
2. Find all "PIZZA BY THE SLICE" line items with Discount Amount = $3.25 (this discount = the free pizza reward; regular pizza sold at $3.00 with $3.25 discount representing the campaign benefit value)
3. A confirmed redemption = a Trans ID that appears in BOTH sets above
4. Redemption rate = confirmed redemptions ÷ qualifying gas transactions

Note on discount amount: The $3.25 discount on a $3.00 pizza reflects the full regular-price value being credited ($3.25 regular → $0 after discount). Pizza transactions with $0.00 discount are paid purchases, not campaign redemptions.

3. Monthly Campaign Trend — Redemptions and Qualifying Gas

Top chart: confirmed free pizza redemptions per month (bars) and redemption rate % (dotted line). Bottom chart: qualifying gas transactions and their share of all gas fills. Yellow shading = May 2026 campaign focus month.

Redemption rate context: TGC May 2026: 9 redemptions out of 1,666 qualifying gas transactions = 0.5%. TExp May 2026: 14 out of 800 = 1.8%.
Low redemption rates suggest customers are either: (a) unaware of the promo, (b) not bringing their loyalty card to the pump, or (c) the pizza is not always being rung as a free item even when the promo qualifies. Near-miss transactions (TGC: 487 · TExp: 212) represent customers who spent $20–$29.99 on gas — a visible reminder at the pump could convert these.

4. Gas Spend Distribution — May 2026

Distribution of per-transaction gas spend for loyalty customers. Green bars = $30+ (qualifying). Grey bars = below threshold. The split between qualifying and non-qualifying reveals the threshold's natural fit with customer purchase behaviour.

Threshold fit: TGC: 72.9% of loyalty gas transactions qualify ($30+), with 487 near-miss transactions ($20–$29.99).
TExp: 75.5% qualify, with 212 near-misses.
A high qualifying % means the $30 floor is already within most customers' normal fill-up range — the promo rewards existing behaviour. Near-miss volume shows the opportunity to capture additional incremental fills with a slight threshold reduction.
