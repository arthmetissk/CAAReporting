⚠ Promotion adoption tracking — July 2026 POS audit pending

June 2026 (the prior period with a completed audit) confirmed only 6 actual bundle redemptions — 3 at TGC and 3 at TExpress — against 1,882 promo sandwich baskets in that month. The basket-intersection method (11 bundles detected in July) will be reconciled against the POS log once the July audit is completed.

The promo sandwich itself sells well (2,294 units in July 2026, -8.2% vs last year), but the free chip + free fountain drink component of the deal is nearly invisible to customers based on June's confirmed audit.
Promotion Effectiveness — July 2026 Context:

July 2026 vs July 2025 shows -9.5% change in sandwich baskets (2,116 → 1,916). This reflects the underlying sandwich purchase habit, not promo-driven uplift — loyalty data consistently shows sandwich buyers are habitual repeat customers. A clean 31-day vs 31-day July comparison makes this the most direct year-over-year read in the dataset.

The core structural issue remains: the $4.99 bundle is not a registered combo SKU in POS, so there is no mechanism to trigger, measure, or reward it automatically.
Recommendations to Improve Measurability & Adoption:
1. Create a dedicated combo PLU (e.g. "SANDWICH DEAL $4.99") — without this, redemption tracking will never be accurate and cashier compliance cannot be enforced.
2. Add visible in-store signage at the deli counter and fountain station.
3. Cashier prompt script — a simple "Would you like to add a chip and drink for the $4.99 combo?" at checkout is proven to lift attachment rates significantly in C&G formats.
4. June 2026 confirmed: 6 redemptions (0.47%). July confirmed count pending.
