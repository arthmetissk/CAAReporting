Executive Story Cross-month inference

The campaign set demonstrates a consistent loyalty and offer-building pattern. May establishes the audience and initial food, beverage, and rewards loop. June expands the offer architecture through a broader set of seasonal programs. July deepens the summer story with buy-downs and water, cooler, and heat-management promotions. August converts the learnings into a focused August performance review across food, coffee, vape, and fireworks.

✓Offer families that repeat across months are more reliable than isolated one-off promotions.
✓Carded customer behavior is the strongest measurement pattern for repeat purchase and wallet expansion.
✓Creative and category relevance should be paired with useful next-step rewards to protect frequency uplift.
